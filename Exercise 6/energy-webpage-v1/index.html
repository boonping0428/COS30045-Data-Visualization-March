Appliance Energy Consumption in Australia

Welcome to our energy awareness portal. In the Australian market, household appliances account for approximately 30% of total home energy consumption. Making informed choices by checking the Energy Rating Label can significantly reduce your carbon footprint and lower your quarterly electricity bills.

Understanding the Energy Rating Label

Look for the Stars

The more stars, the more efficient the product is compared to other models of the same size.

Check the Number

The energy consumption box shows the kWh/year. This helps you calculate the actual cost to run the appliance.

Size Matters

Always compare products of a similar capacity or size for an accurate efficiency rating.

Televisions & Energy Use

Televisions are a staple in Australian homes. While modern LED and OLED TVs are much more efficient than older plasma models, they are also getting larger. A 65-inch TV will naturally use more power than a 40-inch model.
